# 🌲 WILD LANDS

hey. this is Wild Lands, a first-person survival sandbox i made in Godot 4. you spawn on a big low-poly island with nothing, and you have to build your way up from sticks and rocks. it works on phone AND pc.

no shop, no tutorial, no one telling you what to do. just you, a meadow, and a LOT of trees.

---

## how to open it

1. download **Godot 4.3** (or newer) from godotengine.org
2. open Godot -> **Import** -> pick this folder (or the zip, it works too)
3. let it import. the first time takes a bit cause of the character model, don't panic
4. hit **Play**

**screen is black??** go to Project Settings -> Rendering -> Renderer and switch it to **Compatibility**. that fixes it on some devices.

---

## how to play

the whole game is one loop:

1. **grab sticks and stones** off the ground (they're just lying around the meadow)
2. **craft a Stone Axe** (3 sticks + 2 stones) and a **Stone Pickaxe** (3 sticks + 3 stones)
3. **chop trees.** the trunk shakes, chips fly, then the whole tree falls over *away from you*. it leaves a stump and the log disappears after like 10 seconds. you get wood and sticks
4. **mine rocks** with the pickaxe. every hit gives stone and the rock gets smaller until it breaks
5. **craft a Stone Sword** (1 stick + 2 stones + 2 wood). it cuts bushes for sticks
6. **upgrade to Hardened tools** (wood + sticks + stones). they swing faster and hit harder

you don't have to switch tools by hand, btw. **ACT auto-equips the right tool** when you look at a tree, rock or bush.

### crafting recipes

| item | cost |
|---|---|
| Stone Axe | 3 sticks, 2 stones |
| Stone Pickaxe | 3 sticks, 3 stones |
| Stone Sword | 1 stick, 2 stones, 2 wood |
| Hardened Axe | 4 wood, 2 sticks, 2 stones |
| Hardened Pickaxe | 4 wood, 2 sticks, 4 stones |
| Hardened Sword | 4 wood, 2 sticks, 3 stones |

## controls

**on phone**
- left side of the screen = joystick
- drag on the right side = look around
- **ACT** (hold it) = grab stuff / swing your tool
- **RUN** = sprint on/off
- **JUMP** = jump (obviously)
- **CAM** = switch first person / third person
- **CRAFT** = opens crafting
- bottom bar = tap Hand / Axe / Pickaxe / Sword to switch
- minimap (top left) = tap it to zoom: close -> wide -> whole island

**on pc**
- WASD to move, Shift to run, Space to jump
- mouse to look (click first so it captures your mouse)
- left click or **E** = ACT (hold)
- **1-4** = equip stuff
- **C** = crafting menu, then press 1-6 or click a recipe
- **M** = map zoom
- **V** = change camera
- **Esc** = let go of the mouse / close crafting

## the island

the map is 512×512 and it has **7 biomes**:

- 🌼 Meadow (you start here)
- 🌳 Forest
- 🌴 Jungle
- 🌑 Dark Forest
- 🏜️ Desert
- 🌲 Taiga
- 🏔️ Snow Mountains

trees, rocks, bushes, grass, flowers and mushrooms are placed differently in each biome. the desert is basically all rocks and almost no trees, and the jungle is packed with them. the whole world is **generated by code**, so no premade map. same seed = same island every time.

---

## animations

- **first person:** your tool actually swings. axe and pickaxe come down overhead, the sword slashes sideways. picking stuff up dips the camera a bit, and every hit gives you a little camera kick and shake
- **third person:** the character plays real animations (sword attack, punch, pick up) with the tool in their right hand

## want to mess with it?

stuff i made easy to change:

| what | where |
|---|---|
| tool damage, swing speed, recipes | top of `player.gd` (`DMG`, `SWING_T`, `RECIPES`) |
| how many sticks and stones spawn | top of `world.gd` (`STICK_DENS`, `STONE_DENS`) |
| how fast trees fall and disappear | top of `world.gd` (`TREE_FALL_TIME`, `TREE_VANISH_DELAY`) |
| graphics quality | `main.gd` -> `QUALITY_LEVEL` (0 = low, 1 = medium, 2 = high) |
| shadows | `main.gd` -> `SHADOWS` (off by default because they kill phone FPS) |

**running laggy on your phone?** set `QUALITY_LEVEL` to 0 and keep shadows off.

## what's in the folder

- `main.gd` / `main.tscn` - starts everything: sky, sun, clouds, then builds the world and drops you in
- `world.gd` - the big one. terrain, biomes, trees, rocks, all of it
- `player.gd` - movement, camera, combat, crafting, sounds
- `tools.gd` - the axe, pickaxe and sword models
- `touch_ui.gd` - joystick, buttons, hotbar, minimap, crafting panel
- `assets/character/` - the character model

---

## cool stuff about how it's made

- the world is **100% procedural low-poly**, flat shaded. no textures i drew, no imported maps
- the tools are built from **basic shapes** (boxes and cylinders) in code
- **every sound is synthesized in code.** there are zero audio files in this project
- shadows are faked into the ground (baked contact shadows) so it runs fast on phones
